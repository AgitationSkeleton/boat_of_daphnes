description Power Crouch Stab: after a stronger attack (here the Master Sword's slash, power 2) the stab keeps that power and cut type; with the option off it is a plain stab (power 1)
# A slash with the Master Sword (power 2, cut type 4) is the last sword attack; the Hero's Sword is put back and the stab
# inherits power 2 (its own would be 1). The poke is the test's way to a bigger attack than the Hero's Sword's; the log's "atp" is the sword capsule's power when the stab starts.
timeout 300
save 2-Pictobox
options oot_crouch_stab,oot_stab_power
env BLUEWAKE_OOT_LOG 1
size 640x480
start load
wait link
collect swords
wait 20
press B 8
wait 40
poke 0x803C4C16=0x49/1
wait 10
press B 6
wait 60
poke 0x803C4C16=0x38/1
wait 40
hold R
wait 60
press B 6
wait 60
release R
wait 20
expect \[oot\] stab 1 starts.*\(inherited\)
