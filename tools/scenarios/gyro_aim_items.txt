description Gyro aiming per mode: a constant synthetic gyro turn (up 30 and left 40 deg/s) must turn the aim with the hookshot, grappling hook, boomerang, telescope and Picto Box, and do nothing while walking
# BLUEWAKE_TEST_GYRO holds the turn from the first frame; the log's [gyro-test] lines (active flag, Link's facing, the camera's pitch ratio) are read between the markers.
# The player status word at 0x803CA8D0: 0x4000 hookshot, 0x20000 rope, 0x80000 boomerang, 0x200000 telescope, 0x2000 first-person look.

start load
wait link

wait 30
