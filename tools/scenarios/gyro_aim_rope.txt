description Gyro and the Grappling Hook: the yaw turns Link, and the pitch tilts the aim (kept by the gyro: the rope's camera style makes the ratio from Link's body each frame)
# `gyro PITCH YAW` is a constant synthetic turn in degrees a second (up and left positive); the log's [gyro-test] lines have the
# active flag, Link's facing, his body pitch (the aim's) and the camera's pitch ratio. 0x20000 in the status word at 0x803CA8D0 is the rope's aim.
save 2-Pictobox
env BLUEWAKE_TEST_GYRO_MODE 0
start load
wait link
give grappling_hook
wait 30
equip X grappling_hook
wait 20
press X 10
wait 90
peek 0x803CA8D0/4 rope
log GYRO rope_still begin
wait 60
log GYRO rope_still end
gyro 0 40
log GYRO rope_yaw begin
wait 90
log GYRO rope_yaw end
gyro -30 0
log GYRO rope_down begin
wait 90
log GYRO rope_down end
shot rope_down
gyro 30 0
log GYRO rope_up begin
wait 150
log GYRO rope_up end
shot rope_up
gyro off
press B 10
wait 60
expect_peek rope & 0x20000
