description In-between frames while Link swims across open water (ripples, wake, splash): logs where everything is on screen and dumps pictures (tools/interpjitter.py, tools/interpimages.py)
timeout 2400
save 2-Pictobox
size 640x360
env SA2_INTERP_TEST 0.5
env SA2_INTERP_STEPS 4
env SA2_INTERP_JITTER interp_jitter.log
env SA2_INTERP_DUMP_LABELS 1,2
env SA2_DUMP_DIR jitter_dump
env SA2_DUMP_MAX 6000
env SA2_INTERP_DUMP_BASE 1
start load
wait link
teleport sea 11 -2192 6 -202315 0
wait link
wait 60
stick 0 100 120
wait 120
jitter swim
stick 0 100 400
wait 400
jitter off
jitter swim_turn
stick 100 100 300
wait 300
stick -100 100 300
wait 300
jitter off
wait 2500
shot end
