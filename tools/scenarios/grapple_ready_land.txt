description BR-0031: the Grappling Hook can be readied (Y) on land: the player status word gets the rope-aim bit
# play.mPlayerStatus[0] (0x803CA8D0): 0x20000 is the rope (grappling hook) aim, 0x2000 the first-person look.
save 2-Pictobox
start load
wait link
give grappling_hook
equip Y grappling_hook
warp sea 44 0
skip
wait 30
console pos
peek 0x803CA8D0/4 before_outset
press Y 10
wait 60
peek 0x803CA8D0/4 readied_outset
shot readied_outset
press B 10
wait 60
peek 0x803CA8D0/4 away_outset
expect_peek before_outset !& 0x20000
expect_peek readied_outset & 0x20000
expect_peek away_outset !& 0x20000
